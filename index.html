Sizoo AI

Teenwear’s AI sizing assistant that turns height, weight and real return feedback into smarter size recommendations.

How it works

Sizoo is part of Teenwear and was built to solve one of fashion ecommerce’s most frustrating problems: choosing the wrong size. A customer enters their height and weight, and the AI recommends the size most likely to fit. But it goes beyond a static size chart. Sizoo is connected to Teenwear’s return portal, where it follows customer feedback and return reasons and uses that real-world data to make future recommendations more precise. The result is a smarter sizing experience that helps customers choose with more confidence and is designed to reduce size-related returns.

AI Try-on

An in-house AI experience that lets customers see Teenwear products on themselves before buying.

How it works

AI Try-on turns a product page into something personal. A customer uploads a photo, chooses a Teenwear product and our AI generates a visual of how that piece could look on them. We developed the experience in-house to remove some of the imagination gap from online fashion. Instead of trying to picture an outfit on a model, the customer can picture it on themselves. That makes the decision easier, builds more confidence before checkout and supports sales by helping customers connect with the product before they buy it.
