About me

I’ve generated over €800,000 in cumulative revenue since I started building businesses.

When I was 12, I started designing websites simply because I loved making things on the internet. I had no idea where it would lead. Before long, I was building sites for my first clients and learning how to turn ideas into things people could actually use.

At 18, I launched Teenwear.eu as a small ecommerce experiment. It gradually grew into an international fashion business and forced me to learn every part of building a company hands-on, from sourcing and brand strategy to marketing, operations, customer experience and growth.

Today, I’m still scaling Teenwear while exploring new ideas at the intersection of ecommerce, technology and AI.

The story

Teenwear started in December 2022 with a simple question: could I build an ecommerce brand from scratch? I launched it at 18 and ended up learning every part of the business hands-on, from sourcing and product selection to brand, performance marketing, customer experience, logistics and operations.

Sizoo AI

Teenwear’s AI sizing assistant that turns height, weight and real return feedback into smarter size recommendations.
How it works

Sizoo was built to solve one of fashion ecommerce’s most frustrating problems: choosing the wrong size. It combines customer height, weight, return reasons and feedback to make recommendations more precise over time.

AI Try-on

An in-house AI experience that lets customers see Teenwear products on themselves before buying.
How it works

AI Try-on turns a product page into something personal. A customer uploads a photo, chooses a Teenwear product and the AI generates a visual of how that piece could look on them.
